Get help with Chapbook

Chapbook is a cipher game for iPhone, built from sentences in old books. It needs iOS 17 or later. If something does not work or does not make sense, write to the developer and say what you saw.

Writing in

Email contact@gmarcacompaniacreativaltda.com. A message is easier to answer when it says:

  • which iPhone you have and which iOS version it runs (Settings, General, About);
  • the version of Chapbook, shown on its About screen;
  • what you were doing and what happened instead;
  • for a problem with a phrase, the first words of the phrase and the name of its author.

Questions that come up

Where is my progress kept? On your iPhone, in the storage iOS reserves for Chapbook. There is no account and no server, so the app cannot carry progress to another device. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on, a restore of your iPhone from that backup brings the files back with it.

I deleted Chapbook and my collection is empty. Deleting the app erases what it saved. Restoring the iPhone from a backup made earlier may return it.

How do I start a phrase over? Open the menu of the phrase and choose Clear the page. Your letters and pencil marks for that phrase are removed; the phrase itself stays.

A phrase has a wrong word, author or date. Email the first words of the phrase and what you think is wrong. The developer checks the phrase against its source and, when you are right, corrects it in a later version.

Which signs does Chapbook use? Letters or Numbers, chosen in Settings. In a puzzle, tap a sign, then a letter: every copy of that sign changes together.

Does Chapbook need an internet connection? No. The phrases are inside the app and it makes no network connections.

Where is the privacy information? On the Privacy notice page of this site, which About opens too.
